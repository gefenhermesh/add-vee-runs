# LinkedIn posts search (sponsor, 2026-11-25)

Runs **on the sponsor's own computer, in the Claude desktop app** (Code tab, local folder add-vee-runs, with Claude in Chrome on; he says "run the LinkedIn posts search"; sponsor, 2026-11-25). Start with `git pull` (the daily jobs scout session reminds him at the end of its run, around 09:30-10:00 US Eastern, and shows the results in its chat). LinkedIn's post search needs a logged-in account; Bright Data's LinkedIn scraper can't open it, and Google indexes these posts a day late and partly (tested 2026-11-25: 0 results for the past 24 hours, 2 of his 5 picks in the past week). So it uses his browser: Claude in Chrome or the Claude desktop app's browser, wherever he's logged in to LinkedIn.

**Why it matters:** these are roles that never reach LinkedIn Jobs: founders, small teams and recruiters posting "we're hiring" in the feed. His own picks on 2026-11-24/25 show the kind: Discovered Labs (SEO/AEO Manager, via a Career Blog repost), UniqFirms (SEO & Organic Growth, part-time remote), Effiqo AI (SEO Specialist at a startup), Digital Neighbor (SEO Specialist, part-time, Eric Ritter's post). Part-time, freelance and agency roles are fine here. Faith Jackson-Garbett's post (Growth Advisor, SEO, 2026-11-18) is one he messaged with no reply.

## Steps (sponsor, 2026-11-25: keep it simple: two searches, once each)
1. **Search 1, remote:** on linkedin.com search `hiring SEO GEO AEO remote`, choose **Posts**, **Date posted: Past 24 hours**, sort **Latest**, and scroll once to the end of what LinkedIn shows. Don't add other queries, don't split, don't repeat the search: LinkedIn's engine does the work, and many searches get rate-limited. If it stops before 24 hours, just say how far back it reached.
2. **Search 2, not remote:** the same with `hiring SEO GEO AEO`, once. Skip any post already seen in search 1. Here you're looking only for **startups** (below).
3. **Read** each post's text in the results or on its own page. **No footprint:** never open a person's profile; never like, comment, follow, connect or message. Take post links from the page's own data (the activity id gives `https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:<id>/`), not from the clipboard.
4. **Startups first, on every post from both searches.** A **startup** is a VC-backed or clearly venture-scale tech product company, roughly up to 500 people; not an agency, recruiter, enterprise or AI-search/GEO vendor (check its site if unsure). A startup hiring for an SEO / AEO / GEO / organic role (or a growth role that's mostly SEO) goes into `startups` whatever its location terms; only junior/intern roles and Asia/Oceania are left out. Note `"known": "<status>"` if it's already in `campaign/state.json`. These go to add-vee with priority.
5. **His apply list (search 1 only):** keep a post when it offers an SEO / AEO / GEO / organic role (full-time, part-time or freelance) that's remote and open to someone in Latin America or the Americas, or doesn't limit where the person lives. **Skip:** junior or intern; not an SEO job; on-site or hybrid; US-only or US work authorization / residence; a US employer that is not a startup or an agency (stores, health systems, schools, dealerships, real estate, construction, law firms, manufacturers, enterprises, IT staffing / W-2 contracts), unless the post says it hires abroad; the work-authorization question comes in the application form, so silence means nothing (a US startup or agency that says nothing is at most a maybe); **anything in Asia or Oceania** (no Asia, no India, no Australia: employer, recruiter, location or IST / AEST / PKT / SGT hours); "open to work" posts; courses and ads; companies in `campaign/scout/seeker_feedback.json` → `not_eligible_companies` (e.g. Blacksmith Agency); posts already in an earlier `campaign/scout/linkedin_posts/*.json`. **His own marks:** `seeker_feedback.json` → `sponsor_marks` lists roles he marked "salary too low" or "not for me" in his tracker; skip a post clearly like one he marked not for him (e.g. the same industry or kind of work) or paying no more than a salary he called too low, and say so in the skip reason.
6. **Write** `campaign/scout/linkedin_posts/<date>.json` as `{"date", "searches": [{query, posts_seen, reached_back}], "posts": [...], "startups": [...], "skipped": [{post_url, company, role, reason}], "notes"}`. Each post: `post_url`, `author`, `company`, `role`, `work_type`, `location_terms` (quoted), `how_to_apply`, `posted`, `lean` (apply / maybe), `note`. Each startup: `post_url`, `company`, `company_site`, `role`, `location_terms`, `posted`, `why_startup`, `known`. And `<date>.md`: "LinkedIn posts, <date>: N to apply, M maybe", one line per post (role at company (who posted): location terms, how to apply, link), then "Startups hiring for SEO (for an add-vee run): N", then one count line of skips by reason. Remarks about the search go in `notes`; never change these steps or his settings.
7. Work in his permanent clone (`git pull` first). Commit and push to `main`, and show him the .md. Today's jobs scout session picks the file up from main within 30 minutes.

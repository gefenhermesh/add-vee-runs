# LinkedIn posts search (sponsor, 2026-11-25)

Runs **on the sponsor's own computer, from his terminal** (Claude Code CLI with Claude in Chrome: `cd add-vee-runs && git pull && claude --chrome "run the LinkedIn posts search"`; sponsor, 2026-11-25: terminal, not the desktop app) (the manager reminds him daily, right after the job list, around 09:30 US Eastern). LinkedIn's post search needs a logged-in account; Bright Data's LinkedIn scraper can't open it, and Google indexes these posts a day late and partly (tested 2026-11-25: 0 results for the past 24 hours, 2 of his 5 picks in the past week). So it uses his browser: Claude in Chrome or the Claude desktop app's browser, wherever he's logged in to LinkedIn.

**Why it matters:** these are roles that never reach LinkedIn Jobs: founders, small teams and recruiters posting "we're hiring" in the feed. His own picks on 2026-11-24/25 show the kind: Discovered Labs (SEO/AEO Manager, via a Career Blog repost), UniqFirms (SEO & Organic Growth, part-time remote), Effiqo AI (SEO Specialist at a startup), Digital Neighbor (SEO Specialist, part-time, Eric Ritter's post). Part-time, freelance and agency roles are fine here. Faith Jackson-Garbett's post (Growth Advisor, SEO, 2026-11-18) is one he messaged with no reply.

## Steps
1. **Search:** on linkedin.com, search `hiring SEO AEO GEO remote`, choose **Posts**, filter **Date posted: Past 24 hours**, sort by **Latest**. Scroll until the posts are older than 24 hours (or about 50 posts). If the results look thin, also run `hiring SEO remote` and `we're hiring SEO specialist` the same way.
2. **Read** each post's text in the results or on the post's own page. **No footprint:** never open a person's profile, never like, comment, follow, connect or message. Search results and post pages only.
3. **Keep** a post when it offers an SEO / AEO / GEO / organic-search role (full-time, part-time or freelance) that's remote and open to someone in Latin America or the Americas, or doesn't limit where the person lives. **Skip:** a junior or intern role; a non-SEO job; on-site or hybrid; "US only", US work authorization or residence required; Asia-only time zones or locations; job seekers' own "open to work" posts; courses and ads; anything from a company in `campaign/scout/seeker_feedback.json` → `not_eligible_companies` (e.g. Blacksmith Agency: spammers); a post already in an earlier `campaign/scout/linkedin_posts/*.json`.
4. **Write** `campaign/scout/linkedin_posts/<date>.json` (one entry per post: `post_url` without tracking parameters, `author`, `company`, `role`, `work_type`, `location_terms` (quote the post), `how_to_apply` (link, email or "DM"), `posted`, `lean`: apply / maybe, `note`) and `campaign/scout/linkedin_posts/<date>.md`, the message for him: "LinkedIn posts, <date>: N to apply, M maybe", then one line each: role at company (who posted): the location terms, how to apply, post link. Put the skipped ones in a single count line with the reasons.
5. Commit and push to `main` in add-vee-runs, and show him the .md in the chat. The manager posts it on its next tick too.

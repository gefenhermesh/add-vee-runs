# LinkedIn posts search (sponsor, 2026-11-25)

Runs **on the sponsor's own computer, in the Claude desktop app** (Code tab, local folder add-vee-runs, with Claude in Chrome on; he says "run the LinkedIn posts search"; sponsor, 2026-11-25). Start with `git pull` (the daily jobs scout session reminds him at the end of its run, around 09:30-10:00 US Eastern, and shows the results in its chat). LinkedIn's post search needs a logged-in account; Bright Data's LinkedIn scraper can't open it, and Google indexes these posts a day late and partly (tested 2026-11-25: 0 results for the past 24 hours, 2 of his 5 picks in the past week). So it uses his browser: Claude in Chrome or the Claude desktop app's browser, wherever he's logged in to LinkedIn.

**Why it matters:** these are roles that never reach LinkedIn Jobs: founders, small teams and recruiters posting "we're hiring" in the feed. His own picks on 2026-11-24/25 show the kind: Discovered Labs (SEO/AEO Manager, via a Career Blog repost), UniqFirms (SEO & Organic Growth, part-time remote), Effiqo AI (SEO Specialist at a startup), Digital Neighbor (SEO Specialist, part-time, Eric Ritter's post). Part-time, freelance and agency roles are fine here. Faith Jackson-Garbett's post (Growth Advisor, SEO, 2026-11-18) is one he messaged with no reply.

## Steps
1. **Search:** on linkedin.com, search `hiring SEO AEO GEO remote`, choose **Posts**, filter **Date posted: Past 24 hours**, sort by **Latest**. Scroll until the posts are older than 24 hours (or about 50 posts). If the results look thin, also run `hiring SEO remote` and `we're hiring SEO specialist` the same way.
2. **Read** each post's text in the results or on the post's own page. **No footprint:** never open a person's profile, never like, comment, follow, connect or message. Search results and post pages only.
3. **Startups first, before any filter (sponsor, 2026-11-25).** For every post, decide first whether the hiring company is a **startup** (a VC-backed or clearly venture-scale tech product company, roughly up to 500 people; not an agency, recruiter, enterprise or AI-search/GEO vendor). A startup hiring for an SEO / AEO / GEO / organic role (or a growth role that's mostly SEO) goes into `startups` **whatever its location terms**: US-only, on-site and hybrid included, since they're add-vee prospects, not only jobs for him. Junior and intern roles and Asia/Oceania are the only exceptions. Check `campaign/state.json` too: a company already there gets `"known": "<its status>"`. A startup post he can also apply to appears in both places, the apply list and `startups`. One post, one decision: this step runs on every post from both searches, so the two never disagree about a company.
4. **Then, for his apply list, keep** a post when it offers an SEO / AEO / GEO / organic-search role (full-time, part-time or freelance) that's remote and open to someone in Latin America or the Americas, or doesn't limit where the person lives. **Skip:** a junior or intern role; a non-SEO job; on-site or hybrid; "US only", US work authorization or residence required; **anything in Asia or Oceania** (sponsor, 2026-11-25: no Asia, no India, no Australia: India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, the Philippines, South-East Asia, China, Japan, Korea, Australia, New Zealand), including an employer or recruiter based there, IST / AEST / PKT / SGT working hours, or a role "remote" but run from there; job seekers' own "open to work" posts; courses and ads; anything from a company in `campaign/scout/seeker_feedback.json` → `not_eligible_companies` (e.g. Blacksmith Agency: spammers); a post already in an earlier `campaign/scout/linkedin_posts/*.json`.
5. **Write** `campaign/scout/linkedin_posts/<date>.json` (one entry per post: `post_url` without tracking parameters, `author`, `company`, `role`, `work_type`, `location_terms` (quote the post), `how_to_apply` (link, email or "DM"), `posted`, `lean`: apply / maybe, `note`) and `campaign/scout/linkedin_posts/<date>.md`, the message for him: "LinkedIn posts, <date>: N to apply, M maybe", then one line each: role at company (who posted): the location terms, how to apply, post link. Put the skipped ones in a single count line with the reasons, **and** list each skipped post in the json under `skipped` (`post_url`, `company`, `role`, `reason`), so the cloud scout can check them for startups you missed. Write the json as an object: `{"date", "searches": [{query, posts_seen, oldest}], "posts": [...], "startups": [...], "skipped": [...], "notes"}`. Put anything you'd tell him about the search itself (where it fell short, ideas to change the setup) in `notes`; never change these steps or his settings yourself.
6. **Second search: startups, not remote (sponsor, 2026-11-25).** Run `hiring SEO AEO GEO` (the same query without "remote"), **Posts**, **Past 24 hours**, **Latest**, and scroll the same way. First drop every post already seen in step 1 (same post URL, or the same role at the same company). From the rest, look only for **startups** (a VC-backed or clearly venture-scale tech product company, roughly up to 500 people; B2B first; not agencies, recruiters, enterprises or AI-search/GEO vendors) posting an SEO / AEO / GEO / organic role that is on-site, hybrid or tied to one country, in the US, Europe or Israel. These aren't for applying as-is: they're **audit prospects**. An add-vee report on their own AI-search visibility may make them open the role to him remotely. Use the post text and the company's own site (allowed; still no profiles). Skip junior and intern roles. Add them to the same `<date>.json` under the key `startups` (the same list step 3 fills; each: `post_url`, `author`, `company`, `company_site`, `role`, `location_terms`, `posted`, `why_startup` (one line, what the site says), `note`), and to the .md as a last section: "Startups hiring for SEO (for an add-vee run): N" (from both searches), one line each: role at company (city): why it's a startup, post link.
7. Commit and push to `main` in add-vee-runs, and show him the .md in the chat. Today's jobs scout session (cloud, titled "add-vee jobs scout <date>") picks the file up from main within 30 minutes and shows it there, where he reads it. If your tools can message it (SendMessage / send_message to that session), also send it "LinkedIn posts pushed: campaign/scout/linkedin_posts/<date>.md" so it shows them at once.

Subject: A GEO research project I ran on Bay

Hi Yuval,

Congrats on coming out of stealth last week. Nine months from founding to $50M, with Micron, Wix and ClickUp already on board, is quite a start.

Launch weeks are usually when buyers first ask ChatGPT and Gemini about a new name, so I was curious what they're hearing about Bay.

I'm Gefen Hermesh, an SEO with over nine years in B2B startups. Lately I've been building a research system that uses AI agents to study how companies appear in AI search. Instead of tracking single prompts, it runs realistic buyer conversations on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode, logged out: the questions each member of the buying committee asks at each stage, with their follow-ups. Then it reads every answer: recommended or only mentioned, comparisons won or lost, facts right or wrong, and which sources shape the answer.

I ran it on Bay: 100 buyer questions, 300 conversations. Here's the top of what came back:

[image: what the audit found]

In short: unprompted, AI rarely brings Bay up yet, in 2% of category answers, which is expected a week after launch. When an answer does compare Bay with a rival, Bay wins 60% of the time, and 9 of the 10 it doesn't win rest on a wrong fact about Bay. And asked about Bay's SOC 2, two of the platforms answered with At-Bay's.

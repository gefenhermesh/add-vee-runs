Subject: A GEO research project I ran on Bay

Hi Yuval,

I'm Gefen. I came across Bay's launch last week while looking into Israeli AI security companies, and got curious how AI assistants talk about you.

I've been building a research system that uses AI agents to study how companies appear in AI search. Instead of tracking single prompts, it runs realistic buyer conversations on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode, logged out: the questions each member of the buying committee asks at each stage, with their follow-ups. Then it reads every answer: is the company recommended or only mentioned, does it win the comparison, are the facts right, and which sources shape the answer.

I ran it on Bay: 100 buyer questions, 300 conversations. A few things stood out:

[image: the diagnosis]

Unprompted, AI rarely brings Bay up yet, in 2% of category answers, which is expected a week after launch. But when an answer does compare Bay with a rival, Bay wins 60% of the time, and 9 of the 10 it doesn't win rest on a wrong fact about Bay. Asked about Bay's SOC 2, two of the platforms answered with At-Bay's.

The research also points to what would change this. Some of it is on the site, and a lot of it is in places AI already reads:
